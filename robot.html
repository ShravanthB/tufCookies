About THE COOKIE MONSTER

The Cookie Monster, the one and only successor to our first ever robot, (dummybot as some like to call it!) is fully equipped with 4 motorised attachment outputs as well as space on any side of the robot for passive attachment outputs. It has a gyrosensor, two medium motors and two large motors. We used the blue lego spike wheels (8.8cm) on our ev3 mindstorms robot!

Phase 1 - Prior to Regionals

Our first season, first robot, no big expectations. This robot did what we needed it to do, when we needed it done. Getting through to Nationals, couldn't be done without it. In our three runs, first went good, getting us 190 points. In our second run, all our missions went wrong, 130 points only, this wouldn't be enough. We were nervous, but in our final run, the robot pullled through and got 40 more points in the last 5 seconds, getting us 205 points as our best score. However, as we let our victory phase go by, we realised, we needed more to be able to compete with teams with over four years of experience. Hence, we had to start from scratch and rebuild.

Phase 2 - Start from Scratch

As a first season team, it's hard to know what's right. Making this decision is a big risk, but it can go very well for our team. On limited time, we have completed building the robot and are now moving on to coding. It is a lot easier, the robot is more compact and much more versatile. This can lead to something big...

Phase 3 - Attachments and coding

As we have four different motorised outputs, as well as ample space for passive attachments, we are able to complete many different missions in a single launch of the robot. We decided to build the robot around this multi mission strategy and so we decided to group specific missions together which would be completed in a single launch.
